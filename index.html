How CipherTrackers Works — Read This

CipherTrackers is not a service for everyone. We specialize exclusively in helping victims who have already filed an official report with the FBI/IC3 or documented their scam on Trustpilot. We work within the system — not outside it.

✅ You qualify if: You have a FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) case number from www.ic3.gov, or you have posted a Trustpilot review documenting your scam, or you have an official police/federal case file.

❌ You do NOT qualify if: You have not reported your case anywhere. We require documented proof before we can begin any work.

⚠️ Important: Not everyone who contacts us gets their crypto back. Recovery depends on successful KYC/AML identity verification and complete transaction records. If you cannot complete verification or provide required documentation, we cannot guarantee recovery. We are transparent because false promises help no one.

Qualification

Do You Qualify for Our Help?

We only accept documented, verified cases. Here's what we need from you.

FBI / IC3 Case Number

You've filed a complaint at ic3.gov with a confirmation number. This is our primary intake channel. We coordinate directly with the FBI Cyber Division.

Trustpilot Scam Report

You've posted a verified review on Trustpilot documenting your scam. We cross-reference Trustpilot reports to identify legitimate victims.

Local Law Enforcement Report

You have a police report from your jurisdiction. We coordinate with international agencies to escalate your case.

KYC/AML Verification

This is critical. You must pass identity verification with government-issued ID and provide complete transaction records. Without verification, recovery is not possible.
