Our Process

How We Handle Your Case

Every case follows a strict protocol from intake to recovery.

1

Case Intake

You provide your FBI/IC3 case number, Trustpilot report link, or police report. We verify your documentation within 24 hours.

2

KYC/AML Verification

You must complete identity verification with government-issued ID. No exceptions — this is mandatory.

3

Forensic Analysis

Our blockchain intelligence tools trace your stolen assets across wallets, exchanges, and DeFi protocols.

Verification Requirements

We cannot work with you if you cannot complete these steps. This is not optional — it is a legal and compliance requirement that protects both you and us.

  • Government-issued ID — Passport, driver's license, or national ID card
  • Proof of address — Utility bill or bank statement (last 3 months)
  • Transaction records — Wallet addresses, transaction hashes, amounts, dates, exchange names
  • FBI/IC3 case number or Trustpilot review URL or police report number
  • Source of funds declaration — Documentation proving the funds were legitimately obtained
